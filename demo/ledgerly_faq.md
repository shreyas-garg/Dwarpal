# Ledgerly FAQ

Ledgerly is a fictional invoicing app used as the demo domain for Dwarpal. This FAQ is the
context the faithfulness guard checks answers against. All facts here are made up.

1. **What plans does Ledgerly offer?** Free (up to 5 invoices a month), Pro at ₹499/month, and
   Business at ₹1,499/month.
2. **What does Pro include?** Unlimited invoices, custom branding, recurring invoices and
   payment reminders.
3. **What does Business add?** Everything in Pro, plus up to 10 team members, role-based access
   and priority email support.
4. **Is there a free trial?** Yes, Pro and Business have a 14-day free trial. No card needed.
5. **What is the refund policy?** Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase.
   Monthly plans are not refunded but can be cancelled at any time.
6. **Can I add GST details to invoices?** Yes. Add your GSTIN under Settings → Tax, and Ledgerly
   will show CGST/SGST or IGST on each invoice based on the place of supply.
7. **Which currencies are supported?** INR, USD, EUR, GBP and AED.
8. **How do I export invoices?** Go to Invoices → Export. You can export as CSV or as a ZIP of
   PDFs, for any date range.
9. **Which payment gateways does Ledgerly support?** Razorpay and Stripe.
10. **How do I reset my password?** Click "Forgot password" on the login page. The reset link
    is valid for 30 minutes.
11. **Does Ledgerly have a mobile app?** Yes, for Android and iOS. The mobile app cannot edit
    tax settings; use the web app for that.
12. **Can I import clients from a spreadsheet?** Yes, upload a CSV under Clients → Import. Up to
    5,000 rows per file.
13. **Where is my data stored?** In data centres in Mumbai, India. Data is encrypted at rest.
14. **Does Ledgerly have an API?** Yes, on the Business plan only, with a limit of 60 requests
    per minute.
15. **How do I contact support?** Email support@ledgerly.example. Business plan replies within
    4 working hours; other plans within 2 working days.
